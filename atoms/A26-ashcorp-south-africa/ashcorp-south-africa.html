40.0 — Relationship to Block Box™
Inherited vs. Ashcorp-Specific
Inherited from Block Box™ (unchanged): tone-of-voice principles (warmth first, technical authority second), packaging hierarchy logic (brand → product ID → benefit → spec → regulatory), co-branding/licensing separation rules (Part 5), one-color/low-cost print fallback logic (Part 7), governance discipline (nothing redrawn without sign-off).
Ashcorp-specific (this guide): Ashcorp's own logo mark, its own color system (drawn from the existing logo, not Block Box's navy/gold/orange), its own typography pairing, and its own product-line application rules as a factory-licensed distributor.
Mandatory attribution: every Ashcorp-branded Block Box™ product must carry "Manufactured under Block Box™ license" per Part 5.3 of the Block Box Global Brand Standards — this is non-negotiable and unchanged by this guide.
40.1 — Logo System
The Ashcorp Mark
Primary Lockup — Full Color
The mark: three interlocking hands in green/red/blue/gold forming a triangular badge, paired with the "Ashcorp" wordmark in a bold slab-sans and "South Africa" beneath in a lighter weight — signalling partnership, community, and national reach.
Clearspace, minimum size, and background rules follow the same discipline as Block Box™ Part 1.1 — clearspace equal to the triangle's own height on all sides; minimum 20mm print / 28px digital width for the full lockup.

40.4 — Tone of Voice
Operator, Not Inventor
Ashcorp speaks as the reliable local supplier and account partner — practical, credit-and-logistics literate, service-first. Block Box™ speaks as the inventor/IP holder — Ashcorp never claims invention of the tear-away system in its own copy; it sells and services it under license. Approved: "Ashcorp supplies Block Box™ cornice systems across South Africa, with credit terms and dedicated account support." Disallowed: any Ashcorp copy implying it created or owns the underlying Block Box™ technology.

Page 41 · Logo Options — Round 1 (Abstracted)
Six Directions, Block Box™ Less-Is-More
First pass — abstract shapes exploring the "less is more" direction, further from the original mark.
1 · Single-Line Triangle
One continuous outline replaces the four-color interlock; one accent arc + one gold dot keep the "partnership" idea.
2 · Rounded Badge, Flat Fill
Solid navy square (Block Box™ block-mark language) with a single gold arc standing in for the triangle/hands gesture.
3 · Circle Seal, Minimal Arc
Seal/stamp form for credit & compliance documents — quietest option, closest to Block Box™'s restraint.
4 · Solid Triangle, Three Dots
Flat navy triangle keeps the shape memory; three colored dots reference the original three-hand gesture without illustration detail.
5 · Checkmark Ring
Trust/reliability mark — outlined ring with a single check stroke, suited to credit & account-facing documents.
6 · Hex Monogram
Hexagonal badge with a single gold "A" monogram — most condensed option, works smallest (favicon/stamp scale).
Page 42 · Logo Options — Round 2 (Refined Original)
Four Refinements of the Original Mark
Same triangle silhouette and palette as the original Ashcorp logo — the hand-tangle illustration simplified into cleaner, more corporate shapes. Minimal change, more boardroom-ready. None finalized; pick a direction for refinement.
1 · Refined Triangle, Same Gesture
Original triangle outline kept exactly; the tangle of hands simplified to two clean converging strokes + one gold node — same story, corporate line weight.
2 · Solid Triangle, One Accent Stroke
Same triangle shape, filled solid navy; the four-color hand tangle reduced to a single gold gesture line — most boardroom-ready of the set.
3 · Outline Triangle, Three Nodes
Same silhouette, outline only (no fill); the three hands become three balanced color dots — keeps the original palette, drops the illustration.
4 · Outline Triangle, Plus Mark
Same triangle, rounded joins for a softer corporate edge; hands abstracted to a single "plus/partnership" cross in one accent color.
