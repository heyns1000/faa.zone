Clearspace, Minimum Size & Correct Application

Logo Variants & Scale Rules

Clearspace & Contrast (Part 1.1–1.3)
Clearspace unit: Full height of tear-wave cue on all sides. No text, image, edge, or competing mark may enter this zone.

Background: Deep Navy #0E2148, Value Yellow #F5C518, Warm Paper #EEE6D6, or true white only. Min contrast 4.5:1.

Approved reproduction: Full-color on Warm Paper; reversed yellow-on-navy on Deep Navy; one-color for foil, emboss, thermal, or corrugate.

Part 3 — Typography & Hierarchy

Fredoka & Archivo Locked Type Rules

Fredoka
Primary — wordmark, headlines, packaging front copy. Rounded, warm, consumer-first. Never for body copy or legal text.
Archivo
Secondary — body copy, spec sheets, web UI, legal/technical documents. Neutral, legible at small size. Never for wordmark.
Type Roles (locked): Brand display (Fredoka Bold) — headlines only, never below 24px/18pt. UI/body (Archivo Medium/Regular) — web, app, all running text. Technical/spec (Archivo Regular, mono figures) — dimensions, part numbers. Legal (Archivo Regular, min 6pt/8px) — always Navy on light, never Yellow. Product name in title case always. Labels uppercase Archivo Bold +0.06–0.12em tracking. Justified text forbidden — ragged-right only.
