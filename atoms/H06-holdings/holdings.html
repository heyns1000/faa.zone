The Vision

WHAT WE
ARE
BUILDING

Fruitful Holdings is a social enterprise fusing entertainment with community impact — creating platforms that uplift, inspire and connect. The Fruitful Crate Dance Showcase is the flagship, powered by the Sekelbos Tank artisan marketplace and the Tupperware Going Fruitful distribution network.

🕺

CRATE DANCE SHOWCASE

Global street dance competition giving youth from underserved communities a world stage. Regional auditions → National finale → International expansion. Red Bull presenting sponsor.

🏺

SEKELBOS TANK

Vendor-driven artisan marketplace empowering local crafters, vendors and small businesses with access to global markets. 20% commission model, franchise-ready.

🌍

FRUITFUL PLANET CHANGE

Social impact engine raising awareness on poverty, inequality and unemployment through creative expression. Youth development programs integrated into every event.

🏪

TUPPERWARE GOING FRUITFUL

Repurposing Tupperware consultant networks as Fruitful direct sales agents at housewarming parties, kids' events and community gatherings. Revenue-sharing model for all parties.

📱

TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM

Online registration, virtual auditions, live streaming, POS systems via RA Cellular, data analytics for audience growth and sponsorship impact measurement across all markets.

🎓

TALENT DEVELOPMENT

Mentorship framework connecting dancers to industry leaders. Training in choreography, stage presence, social media marketing, entrepreneurship and financial literacy.
