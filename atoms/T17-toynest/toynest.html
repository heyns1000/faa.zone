01 · Logo

The mark & wordmark

The mark is a rounded cube — a nod to the six-face age system and to tactile, handmade play. Pair it with the wordmark set in Bricolage Grotesque. Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the cube.

Do

Don't

  • Recolour the cube outside the palette below
  • Stretch, rotate, or add drop shadows to the mark
  • Set the wordmark in any font other than Bricolage Grotesque

02 · Colour

Palette

Warm, earthy base (baobab bark + savanna paper) with a single living-green primary. The six tier colours are accents only — used as small coded chips, never as full backgrounds.

04 · Brand device

The six-face cube

The signature device: six faces, one per age mode. It's the product's safety concept and the brand's graphic system in one. Use it as a selector, a packaging motif, and a section marker.
